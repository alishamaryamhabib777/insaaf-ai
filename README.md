## 🌟 Overview

**InsaafAI** is an AI-powered legal assistance platform designed to bridge the gap between complex legal frameworks and ordinary citizens or legal practitioners. Powered by Google's Gemini API, InsaafAI helps users articulate legal issues, perform legal analysis, draft formal court petitions, and access case insights instantly.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🏠 Interactive Landing Page:** Clean, modern, accessible portal introducing the platform's capabilities.
* **📋 AI Legal Intake:** Guided multi-step forms capturing relevant legal facts, timelines, and grievance details.
* **⚡ Deep Legal Analysis:** Instant assessment of legal sections, potential outcomes, and actionable advice.
* **📝 Automated Petition Generator:** Prepares structured court-ready legal drafts and petition templates.
* **💬 AI Legal Assistant:** Conversational Chatbot powered by Gemini for interactive legal Q&A.
* **📁 Case Dashboard & Tracker:** Organize, track, and review ongoing case files and generated drafts.
* **🌐 Multilingual Support:** Designed with multi-language flexibility for localized accessibility.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **Build Tool:** Vite
* **Animations:** Framer Motion
* **Icons:** Lucide React
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (`@google/genai`)
* **Deployment:** Vercel
